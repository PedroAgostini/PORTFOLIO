# Home (single-page portfolio)

Scope: the whole site, one route. Visitor mode: Experience (the work leads), with a Persuade job: turn visits into WhatsApp messages or form submissions.

Audience and job: small-business owners (US + BR) judging whether Pedro can make them look credible; agencies/recruiters checking craft. Action: WhatsApp / contact form; secondary LinkedIn, GitHub.

Proof: more than 30 projects created in total, represented by ten selected live projects. Each selected site opens on a 3D MacBook whose lid opens with scroll while the site scrolls itself on screen. Owner-pinned: strong phrase opening; MacBook mockups opening on scroll with the project auto-scrolling; bilingual EN/PT; Slate Dark palette.

Constraints: no invented metrics, testimonials, clients, or locations not shown on the project sites. Must feel human (photo, first-person voice), not template, not heavy on mobile.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Every client site is launched like a product at a keynote: alone on a dark stage, under one light, revealed in builds. Refuses the dev-portfolio default of hero + card grid + skill bars.

OWN-WORLD: The launch keynote. Charcoal stage (#141414 floor, #1a1a1a air) lit by hard spot cones; one light color, crimson #e60039, reserved for "live" and for the action; #66001a as its shadow. Slide-scale Geist type, one statement per beat, builds word by word; Geist Mono only for spec data (domains, categories, counts). Components are keynote rituals: the opening slide, the statement slide, the product reveal, the spec sheet, the presenter, "one more thing". 4px radius, no cards.

STORY: Visitors understand Pedro is a mid-level web developer specializing in websites for companies in Brazil and the United States, with broader experience building SaaS products and e-commerce. They believe it because selected work is revealed running on a MacBook with a one-click "Visit live site"; then message him on WhatsApp or send the form.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Black stage; a spot cone falls from top center onto a faint floor ellipse. Monumental headline left-aligned at ~6vw, three lines: "Websites and SaaS made to perform, ready to grow." / "Websites e SaaS feitos para funcionar, prontos para crescer.", lights coming up on load. Below it, a concise statement of Pedro's specialization in websites, SaaS products and e-commerce plus two actions: crimson "Discuss a project" / "Falar sobre um projeto" (WhatsApp) and "View selected work" / "Ver trabalhos selecionados". Top bar: wordmark, Work/About/Contact, EN|PT toggle. Bottom center: the closed lid of the first MacBook rising at the stage edge.

FORM: Keynote launch stage, candidate 1 of my ordered list (Impeccable's pick, chosen by the user over the rolled drawing set). Seed key 59b5d78a. Signature interaction: pinned 3D stage where one MacBook rises into the light, its lid opens scrubbed by scroll, the screen powers on and the project scrolls itself; between projects the lid closes and the machine turns to the next. Motion grammar: damped (Lenis inertia, spring UI), lights-up/builds, never snapped.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
